Normal Modes of the Market

A system of coupled oscillators splits into independent normal modes. The eigenvectors of a stock-correlation matrix are the market's version: portfolios that move independently. If the market behaves like overdamped oscillators driven by noise, the lowest-variance modes should revert fastest. This page lets you play with the physics, the data, and what the paper found when it tested the idea out of sample (417 S&P 500 stocks, 2013–2024).

2 Two coupled oscillators (N = 2)

Stocks and bonds as two masses tied to walls by springs k and to each other by a coupling spring k꜀. Any motion is a mix of two normal modes: in-phase (the coupling never stretches, so it is slow) and out-of-phase (the coupling stretches, so it is stiffer and faster). Drag a mass to set it moving, or use the buttons.

Bottom plot: displacement of each mass (solid) and the two modal coordinates \(q = V^\top x\) (dashed). Pushing only one mass excites both modes at once, and the energy beats back and forth between the masses.

From springs to correlation

For two standardized assets the correlation matrix is \(C=\begin{pmatrix}1&\rho\\\rho&1\end{pmatrix}\). Its eigenvectors are always \((1,1)/\sqrt2\) and \((1,-1)/\sqrt2\), the same shapes as the two oscillator modes, with variances \(\lambda_{1,2}=1\pm\rho\). Move ρ, or replay the real stock–bond history.

3 N oscillators: beads on a string

N equal beads on a string fixed at both ends. The stiffness matrix is tridiagonal, and its modes are standing waves \(v_{ik}=\sin\frac{ik\pi}{N+1}\) with frequencies \(\omega_k=2\sin\frac{k\pi}{2(N+1)}\). Pick one mode to see it on its own, or All to see a random mix of all of them. Thin coloured lines show each mode's share; the thick line is their sum.

6 Where does mean reversion live?

The overdamped oscillator model (an Ornstein–Uhlenbeck market) gives \(\Sigma=\tfrac{s^2}{2}K^{-1}\), so \(\lambda_k = s^2/2\omega_k^2\): big λ means a soft spring and slow trending; small λ means a stiff spring and fast reversion.

The paper traded a reversion signal on every mode out of sample (2013–2024, before costs) and grouped the results by position in the spectrum, relative to each month's Marchenko–Pastur edge.

Can machine learning tell which modes will revert?

Walk-forward models trained on eigenvalue separation, half-life, autocorrelation, eigenvector stability, volatility, concentration and regime features. Out-of-sample AUC with 95% confidence intervals from a bootstrap over episodes (0.5 = coin flip).

7 Strategy lab

Walk-forward, market-neutral strategies built from the modes, out of sample from January 2013. Choose strategies, set the trading cost, and shade the stress regime found by the hidden Markov model. Everything below is recomputed in your browser from the daily gross PnL and turnover.

Break-even = the cost per unit turnover at which net return is zero. The regime overlay's form was chosen after seeing that reversion pays in stress and loses in calm markets (disclosed in the paper), so treat its results with extra scepticism.
